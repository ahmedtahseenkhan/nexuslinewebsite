Capability, in depth

Not a form builder. A real lifecycle for every discipline.

Risk Register

A statement, not a title

Cause, event and consequence; impact scored per dimension; inherent → residual → target on your own matrix; appetite per risk category. Every score change carries a written rationale.

Operational Risk

Full Basel toolkit

RCSA campaigns, KRIs with defined formulas, amber/red escalation to a named owner and live data feeds, and the loss database — mapped to the seven Basel event types.

Scenario & Capital

Basel III SMA capital

Structured scenario workshops feeding a Standardised-Approach operational-risk capital calculation — the fourth leg of Basel ORM, computed for you.

Risk Quantification

Monte Carlo simulation

Ten-thousand-iteration simulations turning a scenario into a P10 / P50 / P90 loss-exposure curve. Boards understand money, not colours.

Model Risk

SR 11-7 validation

Inventory and independent-validation lifecycle for IFRS 9 ECL, credit-scoring, AML and AI/ML models — with materiality and overdue-validation tracking.

Risk Candidates

From assets to a hierarchy

Generate risks from your asset register into a review queue — forty servers running Payments become one candidate, not forty risks — then roll them up enterprise → category → scenario.

Framework Library

Seventeen standards, one click

ISO 27001, NIST CSF 2.0 and SP 800-53, PCI DSS 4.0, SOC 2, Basel, Shariah and four SBP packs — 1,333 clauses, and control frameworks bring their controls with them.

Compliance

Map once, prove everywhere

Suggested clauses for the controls you wrote, crosswalk suggestions between frameworks, and a Statement of Applicability exported to XLSX or PDF.

ICFR

The annual cycle

Process universe → risk-control matrix → design & operating tests → deficiency evaluation, from simple deficiency to material weakness.

Internal Audit

Universe to follow-up

Risk-based audit universe, engagements, working-paper procedures and findings — tracked enterprise-wide until every one is closed.

Control Testing

Tested, reviewed, proven

A workpaper per test — period, sample, exceptions, conclusion, evidence — approved by a second person before it moves effectiveness. A failed test opens an issue by itself.

Continuous Monitoring

Your tools feed the proof

SIEMs, identity platforms and scripts post control-check results; each lands as evidence and a test run, and a failure raises an alert — never a rating on its own.

Policy Management

Draft to acknowledgment

Draft, review, approve, publish — four-eyes at each step — with the approving committee, periodic reviews and acknowledgement tracked by role.

Board & Committees

Meetings to board packs

Committees, meetings and decisions — and a board pack (PDF and spreadsheet) generated from the live register, automatically before each meeting.

Whistleblowing

Confidential intake

Anonymous disclosure intake with tokenised case references, triage and investigation case files through to a substantiated outcome.

Declarations

Conflicts & conduct

Periodic conflict-of-interest, gifts, personal-account-dealing and outside-employment declaration campaigns with reviewer sign-off.

Delegation of Authority

Authority matrix

A documented delegation-of-authority matrix and dual-control rules — who may approve what, by role and amount band.

ESG

Green Banking

Green Banking Guideline tracking and environmental risk ratings for credit and vendor exposures.

Incidents

Detection to closure

Severity, a minute-level timeline, MTTD/MTTR and links to the risks, assets and controls involved — with the regulator’s notification deadline counting down.

Business Continuity

Plans & exercises

Continuity plans with a recurring exercise calendar and tested recovery strategies — sign-off tracked, never assumed.

Business Impact Analysis

RTO / RPO / MTPD

Structured BIA capturing recovery objectives and single points of failure, feeding directly into continuity plans.

Issues & Actions

One CAPA register

Every finding from every module lands in one shared issue register with owners, due dates and a common lifecycle to closure.

Vulnerabilities

Findings to patch

Scanner findings with severity-driven remediation SLAs (critical in 7 days) and a patch-deployment pipeline through to deployed.

Approvals

Enforced four-eyes

Maker-checker where the requester can never approve their own request, each checker votes once, and every rejection carries a reason — decided in the app or from the inbox.

Built for the working day

Work finds the person who owns it.

First line, second line, internal audit and the board each see their own work — and act on it without hunting through modules.

My Work

One list per person

Decisions waiting for you, what you own that is due in the next fortnight, and policies to acknowledge. First-line staff land here, not in a sidebar of every module.

Alerts

Addressed, not broadcast

Each alert goes to the record’s accountable owner or role and opens the record itself. E-mail digests carry only your own items.

Decide by e-mail

Approve from the inbox

Approve and Reject buttons in the e-mail, on single-use 72-hour links, under the same four-eyes rules. Switch it off where policy requires two-factor for every approval.

Navigation

A sidebar for your job

People see only what their permissions allow, with groups opened for first line, second line, internal audit or the board.

Drill-through

Every number opens its list

A dashboard count opens its register filtered to the same rows. Bulk-set owners, dates and status, with a summary before anything changes.

Onboarding

Live in five steps

Locale, frameworks and modules — pre-selected for your institution type, with Shariah governance for an Islamic bank — then invite the team.

Record depth

Every core record, examiner-deep.

What the examiner opens — and what they find inside it.

Incidents

The regulator’s clock, to the minute

A timeline in minutes, the notification deadline with a live countdown and an on-time or late badge, and MTTD/MTTR. A data breach opens its breach record; a cost becomes a loss event.

Issues

Closed only when proven

Linked to the risks, controls, clauses and vendors they concern. Closing needs an independent validation with evidence, and every due-date move is logged.

Key Risk Indicators

Defined, escalated, fed

Definition, formula and data source; higher-, lower- or within-range thresholds; amber and red escalation to a named person and role; readings posted by your systems.

Third Parties

Tiered, not guessed

Due diligence, data residency, fourth parties and certifications with expiry alerts. An eight-question tiering questionnaire sets the inherent tier — overriding it needs a reason.

Policies

Approved, superseded, acknowledged

An approving committee, an effective date, the policy it supersedes — retired automatically on publication — and who in each role has acknowledged it.

Record Pages

Every record explains itself

Each record opens as a dossier: a plain-language headline, a summary band, open points each with a link to fix it, and the sign-off trail alongside.
